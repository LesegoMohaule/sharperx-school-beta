Android companion

Write on your SharperTime canvas with your phone.

Use your Android phone as a touch and stylus surface for the canvas open on your computer. Your strokes appear live on that canvas, where you can review and submit the work.

If an earlier test build was signed with a different certificate, Android cannot update it in place. Uninstalling that build may remove its local data.

Install and connect

  1. Tap Download SharperTime Canvas and wait for the APK download to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded APK from your browser's download notification or the Downloads folder. Android opens its installer; tap Install.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, return to the installer, and confirm. You can switch that permission off again after installation.
  4. On your computer, open a canvas and choose Use phone. Scan its QR code to fill the code and computer address automatically. If you enter the six-digit code manually for a local session, also enter the computer address shown beside it.
Pairing keeps your account session on the computer. SharperTime Canvas receives a short-lived canvas connection and does not sign in as a second device. The computer remains responsible for reviewing and submitting the work.
SharperTime Canvas is installed directly from this website. Android will ask you to approve the installation.
