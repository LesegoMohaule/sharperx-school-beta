Inside the lesson

Four connected ideas, one worked example.

The sample is designed to be short enough for revision and structured enough to lead into practice.

01

Derivative as gradient

Move from average gradient on a secant line to the instantaneous gradient on a tangent.

02

Power rule

Differentiate polynomial terms efficiently and connect the rule to the derivative function.

03

Tangent equation

Use a point and a gradient to write the equation of a tangent line.

04

Stationary points

Set the derivative to zero, calculate the points and classify the turning behaviour.
