# SharperTime website copy

## Site title

SharperTime - Practical AI Mathematics Learning

## Hero

Make difficult maths feel workable.

SharperTime is a focused AI learning platform for Grade 12 Mathematics. It helps a learner ask a question, practise at the right difficulty, get a hint, submit their work and understand the correction.

Launch status: The platform is launching soon. Registration and paid access will open when the launch is ready.

Buttons: Watch the product demo / Explore the application / See launch status

## Product promise

The learner stays in the loop.

SharperTime combines multimodal input, guided practice, diagnostic marking and short worked-example lessons in one learning flow. The learner does the work; the platform provides structure, optional support and a useful next step.

## Product loop

- Ask naturally: typed mathematics, voice, textbook photos and handwritten graphs.
- Practise with intent: choose a topic and difficulty before attempting a question.
- Get diagnostic marking: understand the error, not only the final answer.
- Learn in context: return to a short lesson connected to the concept.

## Evidence

The website includes three review pages: a five-minute product demonstration, a Grade 12 Differential Calculus lesson, and the Google Africa Applied AI Lab application deck in PDF and PowerPoint formats.

## Roadmap

Grade 12 Mathematics first. Prove the learning loop, launch locally, measure what helps, then expand curriculum coverage, devices and live tutoring capabilities from evidence.
