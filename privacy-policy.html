Privacy Policy

SharperTime processes account details, contact details, ID number, address details, email verification status, payment references, app usage, uploads and support messages to provide and secure the service.

Passwords are stored as hashes. Card details are handled by PayFast and are not stored by SharperTime. Email OTPs and password reset tokens are stored only as hashes and expire.

The platform is launching soon, and registration is currently closed. The registration form will be enabled when the launch is ready.

SharperTime may keep records needed for account support, payment reconciliation, fraud prevention, security logs and legal obligations. You may contact support to ask about your account data.
