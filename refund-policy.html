Refund Policy

Payments are for digital access periods and in-platform usage allowances. If PayFast confirms a payment but access is not allocated, contact support with the payment reference so the account can be checked and corrected.

All payments, access periods, initial allowances and pay-as-you-go top-ups are non-refundable. This includes payments where access was delivered or used, an allowance remains unused, or an AI response was not accepted as official advice, a school mark, an examination result or a professional decision.

For payment-support enquiries, email SharperX at info@sharperx.co.za with the relevant payment reference. This contact route does not change the no-refund policy.
