Platform status and registration

SharperTime is launching soon. The public website is available for information and product review, but new registration and paid access are not currently open. When registration is enabled, users must provide accurate information and use the service only through the official SharperTime process.

Services

SharperTime provides educational support for Grade 12 Mathematics through guided practice, hints, diagnostic feedback, automated assistance and lesson-video content. The planned paid access options combine tutor features and lesson-video access; they are not separate products.

Payments and access

When checkout opens, a payment of R35 will provide 30 days of combined Grade 12 Mathematics tutor access and lesson-video access, together with an AI usage allowance with a service value of R5. Tutor access and lesson-video access are one combined service and are not separate products.

The AI usage allowance is an in-platform usage balance for SharperTime only. It is not cash, cannot be transferred and cannot be refunded. You may top up the balance by any amount on a pay-as-you-go basis during the month. Top-ups increase the in-platform AI usage balance and do not create a separate access product.

At the end of each 30-day access period, if the remaining usage balance is at least R35, R35 will be deducted from that balance to activate the next 30 days of combined tutor and lesson-video access. If the remaining balance is below R35, access will stop while the balance remains available. You may resume access by topping up the balance until it is at least R35. A balance below R35 that is not topped up for three months will expire.

All payments, access periods and usage allowances are non-refundable. Payments, when enabled, may be processed through PayFast, and a payment is not considered complete until SharperTime receives confirmation through the applicable payment process. The applicable price, access terms and checkout conditions will be shown before any payment is requested.

No official certification

SharperTime does not issue official marks, examination results, qualifications, permits, approvals or professional sign-offs.

User responsibility

You must check automated explanations and lesson content against textbooks, teachers, official curriculum documents and assessment requirements. Do not rely on SharperTime for high-stakes academic decisions or as a substitute for a qualified educator.

Account security

When accounts are enabled, you are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity carried out through your account. SharperTime may restrict or suspend access for suspected abuse, fraud, duplicate identity details, security threats or policy violations.

Availability and changes

Features, content, prices and launch timing may change as SharperTime is prepared for release. Any plan purchased after launch will be governed by the benefits and conditions displayed at the time of purchase.
